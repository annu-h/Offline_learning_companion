# The Little Seed
Once upon a time, there was a tiny seed sleeping in the warm brown soil.
One sunny morning, a drop of rain woke the little seed up.
The seed drank the water and felt very happy.
Slowly, slowly, a little green sprout popped out of the ground.
The sprout stretched up to the sun every day and grew taller and taller.
Soon it became a beautiful flower, and all the bees came to say hello.
The little seed had grown into something wonderful, just like you grow every day!
The End.

# The Brave Little Turtle
There was once a little turtle named Tim who was scared of the big blue sea.
All his friends loved to swim and play in the waves, but Tim always stayed on the sand.
One day, a baby bird fell into the water and could not get back to the shore.
Tim took a deep breath, and even though he was scared, he swam out to help his friend.
He carried the little bird safely back to the sand on his strong shell.
All the animals cheered for Tim, the bravest turtle on the beach.
From that day on, Tim was never scared of the sea again.
The End.

# The Kind Little Cloud
High up in the sky lived a small fluffy cloud named Coco.
Coco noticed that the flowers below were looking dry and sad.
The other clouds were busy racing across the sky and did not want to stop.
But Coco floated down low and gave the flowers a gentle, cool rain shower.
The flowers lifted their petals and smiled up at the sky.
Coco felt so happy that being kind had made such a big difference.
From then on, Coco always remembered to help whenever they could.
The End.

# Counting Stars with Mia
Mia loved to lie on the grass at night and look up at the twinkling stars.
"One, two, three," she counted, pointing at the brightest stars she could find.
Her grandmother sat beside her and helped her count all the way to ten.
Every star seemed to twinkle a little brighter every time Mia said a number.
Soon Mia could count all by herself, from one all the way to ten.
"I did it!" Mia cheered, and the stars twinkled as if they were cheering too.
Mia fell asleep dreaming of numbers dancing across the night sky.
The End.
